Child Drawings

Introduction

I believe that the drawings of children in Cyberpunk 2077 contain wisdom. I will attempt to decode their meaning from the perspective of a child, and list my confidence levels in the interpretations.

Johnny + V

This drawing comes in a couple of variations - some colored and some not. Some iterations of this drawing have a texas logo on the character. This appears to be a sketch showing a reflected Johnny and V. The eye lines from default V's cybernetics and the mechanical arm of Silverhand.
